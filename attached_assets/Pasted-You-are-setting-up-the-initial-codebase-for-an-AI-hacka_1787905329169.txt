PROJECT GOAL
==================================================

We are building an AI-powered Inclusive Communication Assistant.

Problem:
Information is often presented in formats that may be difficult for people with different communication or cognitive needs to understand.

Our system will transform complex information into simpler, clearer, and more accessible formats based on individual user preferences.

Core future features include:
1. Complex information → simple language
2. Long information → concise key points
3. Step-by-step explanations
4. Speech-to-text
5. Text-to-speech
6. Language translation
7. Accessibility preferences
8. Personalized communication based on the user's preferences
9. Document/image input
10. Chat/history

These features will be implemented later. For now, ONLY prepare the architecture.
